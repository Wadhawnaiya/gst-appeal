# Demo facts — GST appeal first run

Use this only as a safe sample for learning the workflow.

## Matter summary

- Forum/stage: First appeal under CGST Act section 107.
- Jurisdiction: Gujarat.
- Tax period: FY 2021-22.
- Impugned order: Demonstration order under section 74 confirming tax, interest, and penalty.
- Core grievance: The adjudicating authority confirmed the demand without discussing the taxpayer's written reply, reconciliation, and hearing submissions.
- Procedural issue: Non-speaking order and violation of principles of natural justice.
- Evidence assumed: SCN, reply, reconciliation statement, hearing request, DRC-01, DRC-07, and impugned order.
- Relief requested: Set aside the demand, or alternatively remand for fresh adjudication after considering evidence and granting personal hearing.

## Missing information before real filing

- Exact order number and date of communication.
- Exact demand breakup: tax, interest, penalty, fine/fee.
- Proof of order communication for limitation.
- Pre-deposit calculation and payment proof.
- Annexure numbers and document copies.
- Full text verification of any case-law relied upon.
