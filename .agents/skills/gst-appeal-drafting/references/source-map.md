# GST Appeal Source Map

Last verified by agent workflow: 26 May 2026. Always verify current law, portal instructions and forum-specific rules again before filing.

## Source hierarchy

1. Current CGST/SGST/IGST Acts and Rules from official government sources.
2. Binding courts: Supreme Court; jurisdictional High Court for the taxpayer/state.
3. GST Appellate Tribunal / CESTAT legacy indirect-tax analogies when relevant.
4. CBIC circulars, instructions and notifications; identify whether beneficial, binding on department, or superseded.
5. Other High Courts and tribunal decisions as persuasive.
6. AAR/AAAR only as narrow persuasive support; flag limited precedential value.
7. NotebookLM synthesis is a research aid, not final authority. Verify the underlying source.

## Core first-appeal provisions to verify

- CGST Act, 2017, section 107: appeal to Appellate Authority; taxpayer appeal generally within three months from communication of the decision/order; appellate authority may condone delay up to the statutory additional period; pre-deposit/admitted dues conditions must be checked against current text.
- CGST Rules, 2017, rule 108: appeal under section 107(1) is filed in FORM GST APL-01 with relevant documents electronically or otherwise notified by the Commissioner; provisional/final acknowledgement mechanics and certified-copy/upload rules should be checked on the portal and latest rule text.
- FORM GST APL-01: grounds of appeal and verification are core filing content.
- Pre-deposit: check undisputed dues and statutory percentage/cap applicable to the forum/date. Do not assume without current-law verification.
- Payment defect repair: verify current portal/CBIC procedure where pre-deposit was paid through DRC-03 and DRC-03A is needed for demand-register adjustment.

## GSTAT / later-stage provisions to verify

- CGST Act section 112 and current GSTAT filing rules/forms (such as APL-05 where applicable) before tribunal drafting.
- Current constitution/bench jurisdiction, e-filing instructions, court fee, pre-deposit cap/percentage, certified-copy requirements, translations and paper-book sequence.
- Whether first appeal is maintainable/required before GSTAT or writ route.

## Citation discipline

For every legal proposition include:

```text
Authority | Forum/source | Date | Proposition | Applies because | Limits/adverse risk | Verification status
```

Use `VERIFIED_FULL_TEXT` only after reading the full judgment/circular/notification/order text, not merely search snippets. Use `SOURCE_BACKED_NOTEBOOKLM` only for NotebookLM answers until the underlying source is independently checked. Use `SEARCH_ONLY` for raw discovery hits.
