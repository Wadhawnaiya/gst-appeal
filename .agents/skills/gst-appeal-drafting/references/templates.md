## Grounds pattern

```text
1. Because the learned adjudicating authority erred in confirming the demand under [section/rule] without appreciating that [record fact], as evidenced by [annexure/reference].

2. Because the impugned order is beyond the scope of the show cause notice to the extent it confirms [issue], although the show cause notice was confined to [scope].

3. Because the impugned order is violative of principles of natural justice inasmuch as [reply/evidence/hearing request] was not considered and no reasoned finding has been returned.

4. Because the finding that [department finding] is contrary to [statutory provision/circular/case law], which provides/holds that [proposition].

5. Because, without prejudice, the demand/interest/penalty has been computed erroneously since [calculation defect], and the matter deserves deletion or recomputation.

6. Because the penalty is unsustainable and disproportionate since [statutory ingredients/general penalty discipline/reasonable cause] were not considered.
```

## Filing checklist

- FORM GST APL-01 prepared and signed/verified where first appeal applies.
- Certified/uploaded copy of impugned order attached as applicable.
- SCN, reply, hearing notices, submissions, DRC forms and order annexed.
- Demand breakup and pre-deposit challan/DRC-03/DRC-03A evidence attached.
- Condonation petition included if beyond ordinary limitation.
- Board resolution/authorization/vakalatnama/letter of authority attached.
- Index, pagination, annexure labels and cross-references checked.
- Case-law copies included in paper-book if relied upon.
- Translations and legibility checked.
- Portal acknowledgement/final acknowledgement preserved.
